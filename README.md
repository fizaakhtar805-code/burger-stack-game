## 🎮 About the Game

Burger Stack is a casual stacking game where you drop burger ingredients one by one onto a moving platform. The goal is simple — stack them as straight as possible. The more layers you stack, the harder it gets. One wrong drop and your burger comes tumbling down!

---

## ✨ Features

- 🥬 Drop burger ingredients with a single click or keypress
- 📈 Difficulty increases as your stack grows taller
- 🏆 Score tracking — beat your own high score
- 🌐 Runs entirely in the browser, no installation needed
- 📱 Works on both desktop and mobile

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser
2. Press **Space** or **tap the screen** to drop the current ingredient
3. Try to land it perfectly on top of the previous layer
4. The overhanging part gets cut off each time you miss
5. Game ends when the remaining piece is too small to stack on

> 💡 Tip: The more centered your drops, the longer your stack survives!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — structure

- **Vanilla JavaScript** — game logic

No frameworks, no libraries, no installs. Just open and play.
